## C. Could not verify

1. **A single first detector** of the exploit — only one low-authority source asserts one; uncorroborated by the operator, the alleged detector, or any Tier 1–2 source.
2. **Exact halt block height and exact halt start minute** — neither official operator post states halt times; a widely copied block-height figure (21,955,968) is also cited elsewhere as the relayer-registration block and is therefore unreliable.
3. **Whether any private (non-public) audit** of the Token Hub, CrossChain/TokenHub system contracts, or the `iavlMerkleProofValidate` precompile occurred before October 2022 — only the *absence of a public report* can be stated.
4. **Outcomes of the 7 October 2022 governance votes** (freezing the hacked funds; auto-burn cover; the $1M white-hat programme; the hacker-catcher bounty) as formal on-chain decisions — the proposals are well documented; the vote results were not located.
5. **A standalone, detailed operator post-mortem** with a full incident timeline, beyond the 7 Oct Ecosystem Update and the 11 Oct Decentralized Response — the 8 Nov 2022 Technology Update is a fix list, not a post-mortem.
6. **Final audited funds-flow accounting** — the ultimate disposition of the ~$429M stranded on BSC and of the attacker's Venus Protocol borrow position in a single authoritative statement. (The later liquidation of that Venus position was a market event, not a law-enforcement recovery, and is not recorded as one.)
7. **Attacker attribution** — no public attribution to any individual or group exists in the sources reviewed (E-04: actor unknown); no FBI, SEC or OFAC statement specific to this incident was found.
8. **Any arrest, indictment, seizure or sanctions action** relating specifically to this incident — none was found in the sources reviewed.
